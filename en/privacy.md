## In short

PicBox sorts your photo library into albums by year and month. **Your photos never leave your
device**, and the app never deletes or changes a photo. There are no user accounts, no registration,
no advertising and no analytics or tracking services. The only data that leaves the device are
**coordinates**, sent to Apple's map service to look up place names for album suggestions.

## What data the app processes

### Photos and videos

To detect events, PicBox reads the **capture date**, the **capture location** (if stored) and the
media type of every photo and video in your library. **Image content is not analysed.** Thumbnails
are loaded only for display in the app. All of this happens **on the device only**; **no upload takes
place.**

When you confirm a suggestion, PicBox creates folders and an album in your photo library and adds
the chosen photos. Photos are neither deleted nor moved nor changed. If you use iCloud Photos, iOS
syncs these albums through Apple's iCloud like any other; that is the responsibility of Apple and
your iCloud settings.

### Location data

PicBox does **not** ask for your current location. It only uses the coordinates already stored in
your photos. To suggest an album name (such as "Paris"), the coordinate of an event is transmitted to
the following service:

| Service | Provider | Purpose | Data transmitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reverse geocoding (`MapKit`, system service) | Apple | place name for the album suggestion | coordinates |

Apple acts as an **independent controller**; there is no processing on our behalf, as we have no
influence over its processing. Apple's privacy policy applies: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

We do **not** send photos, capture times, contact details, or any user or device identifiers
assigned by us. For technical reasons, your device's **IP address** is visible to Apple with every
request, just as it is when you open any website. The app stores place names it has found on the
device, so the same place is not looked up twice.

### Transfers to third countries

Reverse geocoding is performed through a system service provided by Apple. Apple also processes data
outside the EU, including in the United States, and states that it bases such transfers on the
European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

Where data is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, there is a risk that public
authorities there may access the data without legal remedies comparable to EU standards being
available. Only coordinates are transmitted.

### App Store

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. When you download or update it, Apple processes
data (e.g. Apple account, time of download, device data) under its own responsibility. We have no
influence over this; Apple's privacy policy applies.

### Data on the device

Settings (language, appearance, name of the PicBox folder, grouping) are kept in the app's
`UserDefaults`. Rejected suggestions (as time ranges) and the place names already found are stored
in a file in the app's own storage. This data is not transmitted to us. Like all app data, it may be
part of your iCloud or computer backup. It is deleted when you delete the app. The albums created in
your photo library remain.

## Legal bases

- **Evaluation of photos and storage of settings on the device:** processing takes place locally
  only and serves the app functionality you requested - Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- **Transmission of coordinates to Apple's geocoding service:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because no place
  name for the album suggestion can be determined without this request.
- **Access logs for the operation of this website:** see the section "Hosting of this website".

## Retention

We do not operate any servers ourselves and do not store your data. Settings and app data stay on
your device until you delete the app; the created albums stay in your library until you delete them
yourself. How long Apple retains access logs is determined by Apple. If you write to us by email, we
delete the message once your request has been dealt with conclusively and no statutory retention
obligations apply.

## Permissions

- **Photos (full access)**: to read the date and place of your photos, show thumbnails and create
  albums and folders. With limited access iOS cannot create folders, so full access is required.

The permission can be revoked in the iOS settings. You are under no legal or contractual obligation
to grant it, and you suffer no disadvantage if you decline. Without photo access, however, PicBox
can neither make suggestions nor create albums.

## Data sharing and tracking

**No tracking takes place.** Data is not used for advertising, not combined with third-party data
and not passed on to data brokers. There are no analytics SDKs.

## Hosting of this website

This section concerns **the website, not the app**. The app itself is distributed through the App
Store; the privacy policy you are reading, by contrast, sits on a web server - and merely opening it
processes data.

The site is provided via **GitHub Pages**, a service of GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street,
San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), a Microsoft company. When you open the page, your browser transmits
technically necessary data to GitHub, in particular your **IP address**, the date and time of the
request, the address requested, and details about your browser and operating system. GitHub states
in its own documentation that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security reasons,
regardless of whether they are signed in to GitHub.

We have no influence over the scope and duration of this logging; GitHub acts under its own
responsibility in this respect. For details see GitHub's privacy statement:
<https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>

The **legal basis** is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in providing this mandatory
information reliably and securely without operating our own server. You may object to this
processing under Art. 21 GDPR.

**Transfers to the USA:** GitHub also processes data in the United States. The company states that
it bases such transfers on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c)
GDPR) and on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. In the USA there is a risk that public authorities
may access the data without legal remedies comparable to EU standards being available.

This site sets **no cookies**, embeds no external fonts, scripts or images, and uses **no analytics
or tracking tools**. It is served encrypted over HTTPS.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal data about them.

## Your rights

You have the following rights: access to the data processed about you (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification
(Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and
objection to processing (Art. 21). Where you have consented to processing, you may withdraw that
consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

**A practical note:** the app creates no user accounts and no server-side database on our side. All
data created in the app resides exclusively on your device; you can inspect, change and delete it
there yourself, at the latest by deleting the app. Since we cannot identify you on the basis of this
data, we are not obliged under Art. 11(2) GDPR to store additional data solely in order to fulfil
these rights. If you write to the email address above, we process the information contained in your
message - in that respect the rights apply without restriction.

### Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority about the
processing of your personal data (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your
residence, your place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.
