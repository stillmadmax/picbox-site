What the app does for you.

Events, not piles of photos

A week in Paris becomes one album, even though nobody takes photos at night. A walk from your front door becomes one too. PicBox tells home from away and groups accordingly.

You have the last word

Nothing is created without your confirmation. A suggestion doesn't fit? Reject it and it won't come back. Tapped the wrong button? Just undo.

Names that already fit

PicBox suggests place and dates as the name, such as "Paris, 10.07.2026 - 15.07.2026". Keep it, or write what the event meant to you.

Tidy up, again and again

Open PicBox every few weeks and you only see what is new. A month's leftover photos, such as single shots and screenshots, can go into an album of their own. How finely PicBox groups is up to you.

Your photos stay where they are.

Read the privacy policy

  • Nothing is deleted. PicBox only creates albums and folders. Photos are never deleted, moved or changed.
  • No image analysis. PicBox uses only the date and place of each photo. What your photos show is never read.
  • Only coordinates leave the device, and only for place names, through Apple's map service.
  • No account, no trackers, no ads. Event detection runs entirely on your iPhone.
